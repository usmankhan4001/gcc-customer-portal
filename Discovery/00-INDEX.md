# GCCStartup Webapp Discovery — Question Groups

## Purpose

These 5 question groups capture the minimum information needed before any
planning, design, or development begins. Answer them in order — each group
builds on the previous.

## How to Fill Out

- **CONFIRMED** = You know the exact number/fact
- **ESTIMATE** = Best guess (fine for now, will validate later)
- **UNKNOWN** = Genuinely don't know (we'll need to find out)
- **NOT APPLICABLE** = Doesn't apply to your business

Rough answers are better than no answers. We can refine later.

## Question Groups

| # | File | What It Covers | Owner |

| 1 | `01-traffic-and-conversion.md` | Website visitors, leads, conversion rates, revenue | You + analytics |
| 2 | `02-lead-quality-and-source.md` | Where leads come from, quality, ICP fit | You + sales data |

| 4 | `04-competitive-position.md` | Competitors, differentiation, win/loss reasons | You + market research |

## After Completion

Once all 5 files are filled out, I will:

1. Run a readiness audit (identify what's confirmed vs. assumed vs. unknown)
2. Identify the highest-risk unknown
3. Generate the Concept Paper
4. Move into PRD and functional definition
