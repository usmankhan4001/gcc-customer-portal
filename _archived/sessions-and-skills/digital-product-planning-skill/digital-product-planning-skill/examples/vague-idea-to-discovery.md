# Example: Vague Idea → Discovery

User: "I want to build a CRM for property companies."

Do not immediately create a CRM feature list.

Investigate:
- What problem existing CRM/processes fail to solve.
- How leads currently arrive, are assigned, followed up, qualified, and closed.
- Who uses the system and who owns decisions.
- Whether it is internal, SaaS, or multi-tenant.
- Whether a custom CRM is justified.
- What success looks like.

Track unknowns rather than inventing requirements.
