# Example: Change Impact

Previous: "The system is for one company."
New: "We want to sell it to multiple companies."

Treat this as a material product-model change.

Review likely impact on:
- tenancy and data isolation
- users and permissions
- onboarding and administration
- billing
- branding/configuration
- security
- reporting
- architecture
- MVP scope
- delivery

Do not merely add "multi-company support" as another feature.
