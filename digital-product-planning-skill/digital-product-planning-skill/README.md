# Digital Product Planning & Definition Skill

Reusable Agent Skills bundle for planning digital products from discovery through
requirements, architecture, technology strategy, MVP, delivery planning, and
readiness audit.

## Install
Upload the ZIP bundle in ChatGPT's Skills area.

## Suggested invocation
"Use the Digital Product Planning & Definition skill. I have a new product idea."

The main workflow is in SKILL.md. Supporting templates and examples are in
references/ and examples/.
