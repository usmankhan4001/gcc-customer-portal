* Annual Nominee / Director Renewals ($750–$1,500/year).
* Annual Trade License & Registered Agent Renewals.
* UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) Corporate Tax Return Filings & Bookkeeping.
* Investor Visa & Emirates ID Renewals (every 2 or 10 years).
* Corporate Actions (Share transfers, adding activities, bank KYC refresh).

### 2. Core Features for Existing Clients

---

#### 🏢 Feature 1: "Bring Your Company" Migration Wizard
* **Target**: Clients who previously registered an entity with another slow/expensive agent, or clients who set up a company with GCCStartup prior to this portal.
* **Workflow**:
  1. Client enters company name, jurisdiction (e.g. IFZA Freezone, Hong Kong, Singapore), and license number.
  2. Uploads existing Trade License, MoA, and Certificate of Incorporation into the Vault.
  3. GCCStartup verifies the entity and takes over compliance/nominee management.
  4. Entity immediately appears in the client's **"My Companies"** dashboard.

---

#### 🔄 Feature 2: Automated Annual Renewal Engine
* **Tracked Expirations**:
  * Government Trade License / Certificate of Incumbency.
  * Nominee Director & UBO Renewal ($750–$1,500/yr).
  * Registered Office & Virtual Address / Ejari lease.
* **Automated Renewal Cadence**:
  * **Day -60**: Friendly early-bird notice on Portal + WhatsApp (*"Your Hong Kong Nominee Renewal is due in 60 days. Lock in your renewal fee today."*).
  * **Day -30**: Official renewal invoice generated with 1-click Stripe payment.
  * **Day -14**: Urgent reminder with penalty warning for government registry deadlines.
  * **Day 0**: Renewal payment collected, government fees disbursed, renewal certificate uploaded to client vault.

---

#### 📊 Feature 3: UAE Corporate Tax & VAT Compliance Hub
* **Corporate Tax (9% FTA)**:
  * Automatically calculates filing deadline (9 months after the end of the financial year).
  * In-portal questionnaire for taxable profit calculation.
  * 1-Click *"Submit Corporate Tax Return with GCCStartup ($450)"*.
* **Quarterly VAT Returns (5%)**:
  * Tracks 4 filing quarters (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4).
  * Drag-and-drop bucket for monthly sales/expense invoices.
  * Tax Agent files directly with FTA and posts payment confirmation challan into the portal.

---

#### 🛂 Feature 4: Visa, Emirates ID & Ejari Expiry Tracker
* **Residency Tracking**:
  * Counts down validity of 2-Year Investor/Partner Visas and 10-Year Golden Visas.
  * Alerts 90 days before Emirates ID expiry to avoid AED 50/day overstay fines.
* **Office Lease / Ejari Tracking**:
  * Reminds clients when virtual desk / physical office lease is up for renewal.
  * 1-Click lease renewal request.

---

#### ⚡ Feature 5: On-Demand Corporate Actions & Secretarial Services
Clients can request structural changes to their active entities directly from their dashboard:
1. **Change of Company Name**: Re-filing and updating registry records.
2. **Add / Remove Shareholder or Director**: Drafting board resolutions, updating share registers.
3. **Add Business Activity**: Expanding license scope (e.g. adding e-commerce to consulting license).
4. **Increase Share Capital**: Formal capital amendment documentation.
5. **Issue Certificate of Incumbency / Good Standing**: Official stamped document for banks and partners.
6. **Board Resolution Drafting**: For opening extra bank accounts or signing major contracts.

---

#### 🔒 Feature 6: One-Click Shareable Document Locker
Entrepreneurs constantly need to share their Trade License, MoA, and Tax Certificates with suppliers, banks, payment processors (Stripe, PayPal), and marketplaces (Amazon, Noon).
* **In-Portal Feature**: Client clicks **"Create Shareable Link"**.
* **Controls**:
  * Set password protection.
  * Set link expiration (e.g., valid for 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days).
  * Select exactly which documents are visible (e.g., Trade License only, or Full Company Kit).
* Eliminates the need to dig through messy email attachments.

---

#### 🏦 Feature 7: Corporate Bank Account Health & KYC Refresh
* Most banks (Emirates NBD, Wio, Airwallex, Wise) require an **Annual KYC Refresh** (updated utility bill, fresh trade license, recent financial statements).
* Failure to respond leads to immediate account freezes.
* **Portal Feature**: The system alerts the client 30 days before their bank's annual KYC due date and prepares a clean, updated KYC package ready for one-click submission.
