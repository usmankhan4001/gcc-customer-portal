### 2. The 10 Interactive Micro-Tools

---

#### 🛠️ Tool 1: The Global Tax Arbitrage & Savings Calculator (`/tools/tax-calculator`)
* **Target Audience**: EU, UK, USA, Canadian digital entrepreneurs (e-commerce, SaaS, consultants).
* **Inputs**:
  * Home country of tax residence (Netherlands, UK, Germany, France, USA, Canada, Spain, Australia, etc.).
  * Annual gross revenue / net profit (€ / $ / £).
  * Business activity (Services, Physical Goods, Digital Goods).
* **Calculations**:
  * Computes home country income + corporate tax liability (e.g. 49.5% in Netherlands).
  * Computes GCCStartup optimized tax (0% Bahrain, 0% HK foreign-sourced, 9% UAE Freezone).
  * Calculates **Net Annual Cash Saved** (e.g., *"You save €92,400 every year"*).
* **Output / CTA**:
  * Visual side-by-side comparison bar chart.
  * CTA: *"Lock In Your 0% Structure — Start Setup"* ➔ routes pre-filled data to Onboarding Wizard.

---

#### 🛠️ Tool 2: UAE Free Zone Qualifying Person (QFZP) 0% vs 9% Eligibility Checker (`/tools/qfzp-checker`)
* **Target Audience**: Companies registering in UAE Free Zones wanting to verify if they qualify for 0% Corporate Tax under Cabinet Decision No. 55 of 2023.
* **Inputs**:
  * Transactions with other Free Zone persons vs. Mainland UAE vs. Foreign entities.
  * Nature of income: Qualifying activities (Manufacturing, Fund management, Headquarters services, Holding shares, Logistics) vs. Non-qualifying activities.
  * Adequate substance in UAE (dedicated office, full-time staff).
* **Calculations**:
  * De Minimis rule evaluation (Non-qualifying revenue < 5% of total revenue or AED 5,000,000).
  * Audit score: **100% Qualifying (0% Corporate Tax)** vs. **Standard 9% Tax Rate**.
* **Output / CTA**:
  * Instant Compliance Certificate & Structuring Recommendation.

---

#### 🛠️ Tool 3: UAE VAT & Mandatory TRN Registration Threshold Calculator (`/tools/vat-calculator`)
* **Target Audience**: Businesses operating in or trading into the UAE.
* **Inputs**:
  * Annual taxable supplies/expenses in UAE (AED).
  * Imports of goods/services into the UAE.
* **Calculations**:
  * **Mandatory Threshold**: Exceeds AED 375,000 (Registration required within 20 business days to avoid AED 10,000 fine).
  * **Voluntary Threshold**: Exceeds AED 187,500 (Can voluntarily register to recover input VAT).
  * **Exempt / Below Threshold**: No registration required.
* **Output / CTA**:
  * TRN Registration Countdown + 1-Click *"Register TRN with GCCStartup ($350)"*.

---

#### 🛠️ Tool 4: Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) & Nominee Privacy Risk Assessment (`/tools/privacy-check`)
* **Target Audience**: Entrepreneurs concerned about public registry visibility, competitor spying, or home country scrutiny.
* **Inputs**:
  * Target jurisdiction (Hong Kong, Singapore, UAE, UK, Ireland).
  * Is public director/shareholder registry open to public search?
  * Need for fintech vs. physical bank accounts.
* **Calculations**:
  * Generates a **Public Privacy Risk Score** (0–100%).
  * Recommends: **Tier 1 (Self UBO)** vs. **Tier 2 (Nominee Director & UBO Shield)**.
* **Output / CTA**:
  * Explains exact legal corporate veil mechanisms and sends sample Nominee Agreement.

#### 🛠️ Tool 5: Mainland vs. Free Zone Decision Matrix (`/tools/mainland-vs-freezone`)
* **Target Audience**: Entrepreneurs looking at Dubai / UAE setup but confused by 40+ Freezones vs. Mainland DED.
* **Inputs**:
  * Do you need to sell physical goods directly inside the UAE local market?
  * Do you need government tenders or commercial retail shopfronts?
  * How many residency visas do you need for staff/founders?
  * Desired physical location (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, RAK, Sharjah, IFZA, Meydan).
* **Calculations**:
  * Instant match algorithm ranking the Top 3 optimal Freezones (e.g. IFZA, DMCC, Meydan) vs. Dubai Economy & Tourism (DET Mainland) with exact cost estimates.
* **Output / CTA**:
  * Itemized breakdown of government license fees, visa allocation fees, and office lease options.

#### 🛠️ Tool 6: Corporate Bank Account Feasibility & Fintech Readiness Scorer (`/tools/banking-checker`)
* **Target Audience**: Clients wanting guaranteed banking before spending money on company registration.
* **Inputs**:
  * Business activity and source of funds.
  * Nationality / residency of shareholders.
  * Projected monthly transaction volume and average balance.
  * Need for multi-currency accounts (USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD).
* **Calculations**:
  * Feasibility score across:
    * **Tier A Fintechs**: Airwallex, Wise Business, PayPal, Currenxi.
    * **Tier A Gulf Banks**: Emirates NBD, First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB), RAK Bank, Wio Business, Mashreq Neo.
* **Output / CTA**:
  * Pre-Approval Probability Rating + Bank Readiness Checklist PDF.
