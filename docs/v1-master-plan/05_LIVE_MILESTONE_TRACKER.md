---

### 1. The Strategy: Eliminating Support Friction

The #1 complaint in traditional corporate services is: *"I paid money 2 weeks ago and have heard nothing. What is the status of my license?"*

The GCCStartup Live Milestone Tracker transforms this anxiety into **complete transparency and confidence** by showing a live, real-time stage progression with estimated turnaround times, official portal routing, activity logs, and instant downloads.

### 2. Detailed Breakdown: Track A (Remote Jurisdictions)

Used for **Hong Kong**, **Singapore**, and **Ireland**:

```
[ 1. Paid ] ──► [ 2. Official KYC Done ] ──► [ 3. Registry Filing ] ──► [ 4. License Ready ] ──► [ 5. Bank Setup ] ──► [ 6. Active ]
  (Day 1)            (Day 1-2)                      (Day 3-5)                  (Day 5-6)              (Day 6-18)            (Day 18)
```

1. **Stage 1: Order Confirmed & Payment Received**
   * *Status Message*: Payment received via Stripe. Order allocated to senior incorporation specialist.
2. **Stage 2: Official Registry / Bank KYC Handshake**
   * *Status Message*: Client completes identity verification on the official government/bank portal and submits the reference number in the app (or via WhatsApp/Email).
3. **Stage 3: Government Registry Filing**
   * *Status Message*: Official incorporation documents submitted to the Companies Registry (e.g. Hong Kong CR or Singapore ACRA).
4. **Stage 4: Certificate of Incorporation & Company Kit Issued**
   * *Status Message*: Company officially incorporated!
   * *Downloadable Files Available*:
     * 📄 Certificate of Incorporation (CI)
     * 📄 Business Registration Certificate (BRC)
     * 📄 Articles of Association (NNC1/MoA)
     * 📄 Share Certificate & Register of Directors
5. **Stage 5: Fintech Bank Account Application (Airwallex / Wise / PayPal)**
   * *Status Message*: Application submitted to bank compliance. Fintech account undergoing standard 10–15 day onboarding review.
6. **Stage 6: Handover & Operational Activation**
   * *Status Message*: All corporate accounts active. Login credentials, debit cards, and banking keys securely delivered.

### 3. Detailed Breakdown: Track B (Gulf & In-Person Jurisdictions)

Used for **UAE (Freezone & Mainland)**, **Bahrain**, and **Oman**:

```
[ 1. Retainer ] ──► [ 2. Security Clearance ] ──► [ 3. License Issued ] ──► [ 4. Visa & ID ] ──► [ 5. Bank Account ] ──► [ 6. Complete ]
    (Day 1)                 (Day 3-5)                     (Day 7-10)               (Day 10-20)            (Day 20-30)           (Day 30)
```

1. **Stage 1: Retainer Paid & Engagement Confirmed**
   * *Status Message*: Initial retainer processed. Trade name choices submitted to authority.
2. **Stage 2: Trade Name Reservation & Security Initial Approval**
   * *Status Message*: Trade name approved by Freezone / DED. Government security screening cleared via official portal with submitted reference ID.
3. **Stage 3: Official Trade License Issued**
   * *Status Message*: Commercial Trade License and Memorandum of Association (MoA) issued.
   * *Downloadable Files*:
     * 📄 Official UAE Trade License (PDF)
     * 📄 Electronic Memorandum of Association (E-MoA)
     * 📄 Lease Agreement / Virtual Desk Ejari (PDF)
     * 📄 Establishment Card (Immigration file)
4. **Stage 4: Investor Visa, Medical Examination & Emirates ID Biometrics**
   * *Status Message*: Entry permit issued. VIP medical appointment and Emirates ID fingerprint biometrics scheduled.
5. **Stage 5: Corporate Physical Bank Account Setup (Emirates NBD, FAB, Wio)**
   * *Status Message*: Official in-person banker interview conducted. Account undergoing central bank compliance verification.
6. **Stage 6: Final Tax Residency & Complete Handover**
   * *Status Message*: Physical Emirates ID delivered, corporate bank account IBAN active, Tax Registration Number (TRN) generated.

### 4. Real-time Activity Feed & Deliverables Locker

* **Corporate Kit Locker**: Permanent Cloudflare R2 cloud archive where the client can view, preview, or download all issued company documents in 1 click.
